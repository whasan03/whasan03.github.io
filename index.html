About Me

Hi, I'm Waleed!

I'm a recent UIC graduate with a background in Information & Decision Sciences and Computer Science, and I'm passionate about building technology that actually makes people's lives easier.

I like being somewhere between business and technology. I enjoy taking a messy problem, figuring out what people actually need, and turning that into a product or solution that works.
